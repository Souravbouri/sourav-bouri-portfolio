01 MY JOURNEY

From curiosity to cybersecurity.

My journey started with Computer Science and a curiosity about how technology actually works.

Programming gave me a way to build things. Learning operating systems and computer systems helped me understand what happens underneath the applications I use.

That curiosity naturally led me towards cybersecurity, where I began exploring networking, defensive security, security labs and practical experimentation.

Now I am expanding that understanding by learning both sides of security: defending systems and beginning to understand offensive security.
